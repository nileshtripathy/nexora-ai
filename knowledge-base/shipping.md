# Shipping Policy

We offer three shipping options within the United States: Standard (3 to 5 business days, free on orders over 50 USD, otherwise 4.99 USD), Express (2 business days, 12.99 USD), and Overnight (next business day, 24.99 USD).

Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after that time, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day.

International shipping is available to selected countries and takes 7 to 14 business days. Customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, or brokerage fees charged by their country.

Once your order ships, you receive an email with a tracking number. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear. If a package has not arrived 7 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support so we can open an investigation with the carrier.

# Payment Methods and Billing

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept cash, checks, or cryptocurrency.

Your card is charged when you place the order. If an item is out of stock and cannot be shipped, the charge for that item is refunded automatically.

A temporary authorization hold may appear on your statement and usually disappears within 3 to 5 business days. If your payment fails, check that the billing address matches your card issuer's records, that the card has not expired, and that you have sufficient funds. Contact your bank if the problem continues.

We never ask for your full card number, CVV, or password by email or chat. Card details are handled by our payment processor and are not stored on our servers. If you see a charge you don't recognize, contact support right away.

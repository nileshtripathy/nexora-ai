# Order Cancellation Policy

You can cancel an order for free while its status is Processing. Open the order in your account and choose Cancel order. The cancellation is confirmed by email, and your refund is issued to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled. In that case, refuse the delivery or return the item after it arrives, following the Refund Policy.

Orders are usually moved from Processing to Shipped within 24 hours, so cancel as early as possible. Custom or personalized items cannot be cancelled after production has started.

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from Account Settings under Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future charges but does not refund the current billing period.

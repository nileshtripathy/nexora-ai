# Orders and Tracking

After you place an order you receive a confirmation email with your order number. You can view all orders under Orders in your account.

Order statuses: Processing means we are preparing your items. Shipped means your package is with the carrier and a tracking number is available. Delivered means the carrier marked the package as delivered.

You can change the shipping address or items only while an order is Processing. Open the order and choose Edit order, or cancel and place a new one. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed.

If an item is missing from your package, or you received the wrong item, report it within 14 days of delivery from the order page and we will send the missing item or a replacement. Guest checkout orders can be looked up with the order number and the email used at checkout.

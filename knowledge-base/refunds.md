# Refund Policy

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include proof of purchase.

Refunds are issued within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Your bank may need an additional 2 to 5 business days to show the credit.

Final-sale items, gift cards, and digital downloads cannot be refunded. Items that arrive damaged or defective are eligible for a full refund or free replacement if you report the problem within 14 days of delivery.

To start a return, open the order in your account, choose Return item, and print the prepaid label. Return shipping is free for damaged or defective items; otherwise a flat 4.99 USD return fee is deducted from the refund.

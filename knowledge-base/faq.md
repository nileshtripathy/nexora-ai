# Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? On the sign-in page choose Forgot password and enter your email. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

How do I contact a human agent? Ask the assistant to connect you with an agent. Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern, and replies to messages within one business day.

Do you offer price matching? No. We do not offer price matching, but items purchased within the last 14 days and later marked down by us may be eligible for a one-time price adjustment.

How do I update my email or delete my account? Go to Account Settings. Deleting your account permanently removes your order history and cannot be undone.

Do you sell gift cards? Yes. Gift cards never expire, are delivered by email, and cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash.
